POS Advanced Discounts

Controlled percentage and fixed-amount discounts for Odoo 20 Point of Sale.

Features

  • Apply a percentage or fixed discount to a selected order line.
  • Apply a percentage or fixed discount to the eligible POS order.
  • Configure per-POS percentage and fixed-amount limits.
  • Keep tax, rounding, invoicing, refunds, and session accounting on Odoo's native engines.

Compatibility

Odoo 20 Community. Requires Point of Sale and POS Discount.
